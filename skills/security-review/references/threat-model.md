# Compact threat model

| Asset or invariant | Actor/capability | Trust boundary or entry | Abuse case | Existing control/evidence | Impact | Mitigation/test | Residual risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Cover identity and authorization, confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy, data loss, supply chain, resource exhaustion, destructive actions, and operational authority only where applicable. Findings require a plausible path and repository evidence. A missing control is not automatically exploitable; an untested control is not automatically effective.
