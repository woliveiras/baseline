# Decision record

Record an accepted, architecturally significant choice whose rationale and consequences must survive the current task. Prefer the repository's existing convention. Otherwise copy the bundled [MADR-based asset](../assets/adr-madr-template.md).

Keep the decision outcome near the top, then preserve context, drivers, viable options, pros and cons, consequences, and confirmation evidence. Include leave-as-is when credible. Link an RFC rather than copying its full exploration history. Do not manufacture an ADR for a routine, reversible, or still-unresolved choice.

MADR 4.0.0 is the source convention: https://adr.github.io/madr/.
