# Evidence types

- `empirical-result`: measured observation with source, method, population, and limitations.
- `engineering-heuristic`: reusable practice supported by experience or theory but not universally proven.
- `product-decision`: chosen constraint or preference for this product.
- `community-inspiration`: idea or comparison that may suggest coverage but is not evidence of effectiveness.

Also label direct repository observation, experiment, primary specification, and inference. Record what evidence would falsify or materially change the decision.
